How it works

From idea to ink

Every order goes through the same four steps, so you know what you're getting before anything is printed.

  1. Tell us the idea

    Fill out the quote form or email us. Send any pictures or files you have.

  2. We design it

    We lay out your artwork and send you a proof to look over.

  3. You approve

    Ask for changes until it looks right. Nothing prints until you say go.

  4. We ink it

    Your order gets printed and we let you know when it's ready.
